What happens after you send this

The form goes straight to my inbox — there is no assistant, no intake team, and no sales sequence. I read it myself and reply personally. If what you describe is not something I can genuinely help with, I will say so and point you somewhere better rather than book a call to find out.

If it is a fit, we schedule the Snapshot. Before the session I ask for four weeks of P&Ls, labor reports, and item-level sales data. That prep is what makes 90 minutes enough to get to real answers instead of spending the call gathering background.

Who this is for

  • Independent restaurants, food trucks, catering companies, and ghost kitchens
  • Operators who can pull a P&L and item-level sales data, even if it is messy
  • Owners who want to know where the money is going, not a marketing retainer

Who it isn't for

  • Pre-revenue concepts with no trading history to analyse — start with the free operations audit or the food truck launch audit instead
  • Anyone looking for someone to run their social media or build a website
  • Operators who want a report rather than changes they will actually implement

Where I work

Based in Louisa, Virginia.
