90-Day Implementation Plan

Week-by-week action plan to implement everything without overwhelming your existing operations. Start seeing results immediately.

Your 90-Day Roadmap

Do not try to implement everything at once. You will burn out and quit by week three. Follow this plan in order. Each phase builds on the last.

Phase 1: Stop the Bleeding (Days 1-30)

Labor and pricing are where most caterers lose money every single event. Fix these first.

Week 1: Know Your Labor Cost

Watch: Module 1, Lessons 1-2 (The Labor Trap, Event Staffing Math)

Implement: Event Staffing Calculator

Do this: Pull your last three events. Run each one through the calculator. Compare what you actually spent on labor versus what the calculator says you should have spent.

Done when: You know your true labor cost per guest for your three most common event types.

Week 2: Fix Your Staffing

Watch: Module 1, Lessons 3-4 (The Full Labor Picture, Building Your Bench)

Implement: Labor Cost Tracking Sheet, Staffing Model by Event Type, Training Checklist for Event Staff

Do this: Build staffing models for buffet, plated, and drop-off. Start the tracking sheet on your next event. Identify two people you want on your bench and start training them.

Done when: Every event type has a staffing model, and you are tracking actual labor cost on live events.

Week 3: Price From Reality

Watch: Module 2, Lessons 1-2 (The Pricing Trap, Full-Cost Pricing)

Implement: Full-Cost Pricing Calculator

Do this: Take your five most-booked menu items and rebuild their prices from actual costs. Include everything: food, labor, rentals, delivery, and your time.

Done when: Your top five menu items are priced from real costs, not from what the caterer down the road charges.

Week 4: Sell It Right

Watch: Module 2, Lessons 3-4 (Service Tiers, The Client Quote)

Implement: Service Tier Comparison Sheet, Pricing Quick Reference Card

Do this: Set up your service tiers with per-person pricing for each. Send your next three quotes using the client quote template.

Done when: Tiered pricing is live and every quote going out looks professional.

Phase 2: Tighten Operations (Days 31-60)

Your labor and pricing are under control. Now attack food cost and event-day execution.

Week 5: Cost Every Recipe

Watch: Module 3, Lessons 1-2 (What Food Cost Really Means, Recipe Costing)

Implement: Food Cost Calculator, Recipe Cost Cards

Do this: Cost out your ten highest-volume recipes to the penny. Do not guess on a single ingredient.

Done when: Your top ten recipes have accurate cost cards.

Week 6: Fix the Menu

Watch: Module 3, Lessons 3-4 (Menu Profitability Matrix, Waste Tracking and Prep Planning)

Implement: Menu Profitability Matrix, Waste Tracking Log, Prep Ahead Planning Template

Do this: Run your full menu through the profitability matrix. Identify your stars, your dogs, and the items you should kill. Start logging waste on every event.

Done when: You know which menu items make money and which ones are dead weight. Waste tracking is running.

Week 7: Plan Every Event

Watch: Module 4, Lessons 1-2 (The Logistics Mindset, Route and Load Planner)

Implement: Route and Load Planner

Do this: Build a route and load plan for every event on your calendar for the next 30 days.

Done when: No event goes out the door without a written load plan.

Week 8: Run Clean Events

Watch: Module 4, Lessons 3-4 (Equipment Checklist, Order Tracking and Follow-Up)

Implement: Event Day Equipment Checklist, Order Tracking Sheet

Do this: Customize the equipment checklist for your operation. Start tracking every order from booking through follow-up.

Done when: Equipment stops getting forgotten. Every client gets a follow-up.

Phase 3: Own Your Numbers (Days 61-90)

You have the systems. Now build the financial clarity to run the business on purpose instead of by gut.

Week 9: Know Your Break-Even

Watch: Module 5, Lessons 1-2 (Profit vs Cash, Break-Even Calculator)

Implement: Break-Even Calculator

Do this: Calculate your monthly break-even down to the event. Know exactly how many events at what margin keep the lights on.

Done when: You can state your break-even number without looking it up.

Week 10: See the Full Picture

Watch: Module 5, Lessons 3-4 (Monthly Profit Dashboard, Cash Flow Projection)

Implement: Monthly Profitability Dashboard, Cash Flow Projection

Do this: Build your monthly P&L dashboard with real numbers. Project cash flow 90 days out.

Done when: You have a monthly dashboard and a 90-day cash forecast. No more guessing whether you can afford the next hire or equipment purchase.

Week 11: Revisit Labor and Pricing With Real Data

Review: Modules 1-2

Do this: You now have 60-plus days of actual labor tracking and food cost data. Re-run your staffing models and pricing against real numbers, not the estimates you started with. Adjust anything that is off.

Done when: Your pricing and staffing reflect reality, not opening assumptions.

Week 12: Lock In the Cadence

Review: Modules 3-4

Do this: Set your ongoing operating rhythm: weekly food cost review, monthly P&L dashboard update, quarterly pricing audit, per-event labor tracking. Put them on the calendar. These do not stop after day 90.

Done when: Every system has an owner (you, for now) and a schedule. The business runs on numbers, not vibes.

After Day 90

The systems do not maintain themselves. Keep the cadence: track labor on every event, review food cost weekly, update the dashboard monthly, audit pricing quarterly. That is the whole game.
