## Structure

### Opening — a specific moment, not a summary

Not "I spent ten years in restaurants." One scene a reader can picture.

> `[A specific night, shift, or decision. Where were you, what went wrong or
> right, and what did it teach you? The more ordinary and concrete, the
> better — a Friday that fell apart is more persuasive than an award.]`

### What the operation actually was

Establishes credibility through specificity.

- `[How many units, what format, what revenue band]`
- `[Which markets — Virginia specifics matter for this article]`
- `[Brands, if you can name them. If under NDA, say "a national QSR brand"
  rather than implying something vaguer than the truth.]`
- `[Team size and what you were directly accountable for]`

### Lesson 1 — `[your biggest operational mistake]`

The most valuable section and the hardest to write. A mistake you made,
what it cost, and what you do differently now.

> `[What you got wrong. The real version.]`
> `[What it cost — in money, people, or time]`
> `[What you changed]`

Readers trust the person who names their own failure far more than the one
with a clean record.

### Lesson 2 — `[what multi-unit taught you that single-unit never would]`

The thing you can only learn running several at once.

> `[Candidate: the point where you stop fixing things yourself and start
> fixing the system that fixes things. If that is right, what was the moment
> you noticed?]`

### Lesson 3 — `[what's specific about operating in Virginia]`

What makes this article local rather than generic.

> `[Seasonality? University-town rhythms in Charlottesville and
> Harrisonburg? Tourism? Labour market differences between Richmond and
> rural Louisa County? Supplier distances?]`

Avoid regulatory specifics unless you verify them at the time of writing —
they change, and wrong detail here destroys credibility with exactly the
audience you want.

### Lesson 4 — `[why you moved from operating to consulting]`

Answers the question every prospect has and nobody asks: *why aren't you
still running restaurants?*

> `[The honest reason.]`

### Close — what this means for the reader

Bridge from your history to their problem, then one CTA to
`/profit-leak-snapshot.html`.
