A directory of Virginia businesses worth calling

This directory exists because finding a trustworthy local supplier, contractor, or kitchen in Virginia is still mostly word of mouth — and word of mouth does not scale past the people you already know. Every listing here is a real Virginia business, submitted by the owner, with a direct way to reach them. No lead resale, no pay-to-rank placement.

It is maintained by Tre Coleman, a restaurant operations consultant based in Louisa. The food-service listings are the ones closest to that work — restaurants, food trucks, caterers, commissary kitchens, and the suppliers who serve them across Charlottesville, Richmond, Fredericksburg, Harrisonburg, and the surrounding counties — but the directory is open to any Virginia business a neighbour would recommend.

If you run one, add your listing. It is free. If you are an operator trying to work out why a busy month still did not produce a profit, that is a different problem, and consulting in Virginia covers how that work runs.
